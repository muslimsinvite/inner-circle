Surah At-Tahrim · The Prohibition سُورَةُ التَّحْرِيمِ

From Private Oath to Eternal Archetype

Across just twelve Medinan verses, Surah At-Tahrim traces a widening moral horizon: beginning inside the Prophet's own household with a gentle divine correction over self-prohibition and confided speech, expanding outward to the believer's duty to shield family from the Fire, rising to the call of sincere repentance (Tawbah Nasuha) and perfected light, and closing with four unforgettable archetypes of womanhood across prophetic history.

01 · Structural Architecture

Four Widening Circles of Accountability

Rather than treating domestic etiquette, eschatology, and prophetic history as separate topics, Surah At-Tahrim binds them together: integrity in private speech and household relationships is the seedbed for standing unashamed on the Day when neither status nor kinship avails.

03 · Moral Renewal & Spiritual Readiness

Guarding the Home & Tawbah Nasuha

Between the domestic lesson (vv. 1–5) and the historical parables (vv. 10–12), verses 6–8 anchor the believer's daily spiritual architecture: sheltering the family through moral stewardship and returning to Allah with unalloyed sincerity.

Verse 66:6 · Preventive Duty

Protect Yourselves & Your Families

قُوا أَنْفُسَكُمْ وَأَهْلِيكُمْ نَارًا وَقُودُهَا النَّاسُ وَالْحِجَارَةُ

Notice the sequence: yourselves first (anfusakum), then your families (ahlikum). Moral guidance begins with self-rectification before extending to spouses and children. The verse specifies angels who are ghilazun shidad (harsh and severe) and utterly obedient—underscoring that no bribery, flattery, or excuse penetrates divine justice.

Verse 66:8 · The Fourfold Promise

Anatomy of Sincere Repentance

تُوبُوا إِلَى اللَّهِ تَوْبَةً نَصُوحًا

Tawbah Nasuha implies repentance that is pure, cohesive, and irreversible—like mending a garment so cleanly it holds together. Verse 66:8 links this sincere return to four progressive outcomes:

  • Expiation: Removal of past misdeeds (yukaffira 'ankum sayyi'atikum).
  • Admission: Gardens beneath which rivers flow.
  • Honor: Immunity from disgrace alongside the Prophet.
  • Illumination: Light running ahead and to the right, crowned by the prayer: "Our Lord, perfect for us our light and forgive us."
Benchmark of Character · Verse 66:5

The Qualities of Spiritual Excellence

In warning the Prophet's household of accountability (66:5), Allah enumerates a curriculum of moral and devotional traits that define exemplary believers across time:

مُسْلِمَاتٍ Muslimat
Submitting willingly to Allah's decree and guidance.
مُؤْمِنَاتٍ Mu'minat
Deeply believing with inner conviction and trust.
قَانِتَاتٍ Qanitat
Devoutly, consistently obedient (echoed for Maryam in 66:12).
تَائِبَاتٍ Ta'ibat
Habitually repentant, quick to turn back after error.
عَابِدَاتٍ 'Abidat
Dedicated in humble worship and devotion.
سَائِحَاتٍ Sa'ihat
Traveling or fasting—striving in spiritual discipline.
