Surah Al-Mumtahanah: Loyalty, Justice, and the Tested Heart

Revealed in Madinah in the charged period between the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah (6 AH) and the Conquest of Makkah (8 AH), Surah Al-Mumtahanah (“She That Is to Be Examined”) establishes Islam’s ethical architecture for allegiance, peaceful coexistence with non-combatants, the prophetic model of Abraham, and legal protections for emigrant women.

Section 01 · Literary Architecture

A Symmetric Ring Linking Private Conscience to Public Diplomacy

Far from a disconnected series of rulings, Surah Al-Mumtahanah forms a tight rhetorical arc: it opens and closes with the prohibition of compromising alliances (60:1 and 60:13), pivots at 60:7 on Allah’s power to transform hostility into affection, and anchors bilateral justice in 60:8–9 before legislating for vulnerable emigrant women in 60:10–12.

Theological Matrix · Verses 60:8–9

Bilateral Relations: Distinguishing Non-Combatants from Hostile Aggressors

Verses 60:8 and 60:9 are foundational in Islamic political ethics and international law. The Arabic particle innama (“only”) in 60:9 restricts the prohibition of political alliance strictly to active belligerents, preserving birr (benevolence) and qist (scrupulous justice) for all peaceful non-believers.

Category in Surah 60 Defining Criteria (Arabic Text) Quranic Ruling Theological Grounding
Peaceful Non-Combatants
Verse 60:8		 Those who neither fight you over religion (lam yuqatilukum fi al-din) nor expel you from your homes (lam yukhrijukum min diyarikum). Permitted & Praised: Show them deep righteousness (an tabarruhum) and act equitably toward them (tuqsitu ilayhim). Inna Allaha yuhibbu al-muqsitin — “Indeed, Allah loves those who act justly.”
Active Aggressors & Enablers
Verses 60:1–2, 60:9		 Those who fight you because of religion, expel you from your homes, and actively aid in your expulsion (zaharu ‘ala ikhrajikum). Strictly Prohibited: Do not take them as strategic/protective allies (an tawallawhum) or confide state secrets out of affection. Fa-ula’ika hum al-zalimun — “Whoever allies with them, it is they who are the wrongdoers.”
Potential Future Friends
Verse 60:7		 Former adversaries whose hearts may turn away from hostility toward reconciliation and faith. Hope & Restraint: Leave room for mercy and reconciliation; enmity is never presumed permanent. Wallahu Qadirun, wallahu Ghafurun Rahim — Allah is Competent, Forgiving, Merciful.
