Surah Ar-Ra'd: Signs in the thunder, stillness in the heart

People demanded a dramatic sign to prove revelation. Chapter 13 answers by turning their gaze toward the world already before them — skies raised without visible pillars, neighboring soils watered by one rain yet bearing distinct fruit, lightning stirring fear and hope, thunder declaring praise, and hearts finding rest in remembrance.
