The Incident of Slander (Al-Ifk) 24:4, 24:11–26

Five-Step Shield Against Falsehood

Triggered by a false accusation spread through careless talk, verses 24:11–26 transform an agonizing communal trial into a permanent ethical standard for how believers receive, verify, and refuse to amplify unsubstantiated claims.

1
Assume Good First (Zann al-Khayr, 24:12)

Believing men and women are asked why they did not immediately think good of their own community and recognize obvious falsehood.

2
Enforce the Four-Witness Threshold (24:4, 24:13)

Accusing chaste women without four direct witnesses carries 80 lashes and invalidates testimony unless followed by repentance and reform (24:5). Without witnesses, accusers are liars in Allah’s sight.

3
Refuse to Pass Along Unverified Talk (24:15–16)

Repeating what one has no knowledge of may seem minor on the tongue, “while it was, in the sight of Allah, tremendous.” The model answer: “It is not for us to speak of this.”

4
Refuse to Publicize Immorality (24:19)

Those who desire that indecency be publicized among believers face severe accountability in this life and the Hereafter.

5
Pardon & Do Not Withhold Generosity (24:22)

Even after deep injury, those of virtue and means must not swear off aiding relatives, the needy, and emigrants: “Would you not like that Allah should forgive you?”
