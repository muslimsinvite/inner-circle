Movement I · Verses 79:1–14

Five unseen oaths open into twin blasts and a single shout upon the open plain

The surah begins with five rapid oaths swearing by angels executing divine commands—extracting, releasing, gliding, racing, and arranging every matter. Immediately, the cadence pivots to the Day of convulsion: two blasts (الرَّاجِفَةُ and الرَّادِفَةُ), trembling hearts, and the deniers’ mockery of returning as decayed bones—answered not by prolonged debate, but by one decisive shout (زَجْرَةٌ وَاحِدَةٌ).

Movement II · Verses 79:15–26

In the sacred valley of Tuwa, gentle invitation collides with supreme transgression

To answer those who doubt resurrection, the surah turns to history: the encounter between Moses and Pharaoh. Notice the bracketing motif of reverent awe (خَشْيَة): Moses offers to guide Pharaoh “so you would fear Him” (79:19), and Pharaoh’s downfall ends as “a warning for whoever would fear” (79:26).

Movement III · Verses 79:27–33

Seven acts of cosmic architecture silence the claim that resurrection is difficult

Having addressed tyranny in history, the surah turns directly to human skepticism: “Are you a more difficult creation or is the heaven?” (79:27). Seven verbs trace creation from the loftiest celestial ceiling down to subterranean springs and mountain anchors—all unified as provision for you and your grazing livestock (79:33).

Movement IV · Verses 79:34–46

At-Tammah al-Kubra divides all striving into two refuges and compresses life to a single morning

When the greatest Overwhelming Calamity (الطَّامَّةُ الْكُبْرَى) arrives, every human remembers what they strove for. Notice the lexical echo: he who transgressed (طَغَىٰ, the exact verb used for Pharaoh in 79:17) and preferred worldly life enters Hellfire, while he who feared the position of his Lord and restrained the soul enters Paradise.

The Two Refuges (Al-Ma’wa) · 79:37–41

Compare the two moral trajectories side by side

79:37–39 · Path of Transgression Tagha + Dunya

فَأَمَّا مَنْ طَغَىٰ ۝ وَآثَرَ الْحَيَاةَ الدُّنْيَا ۝ فَإِنَّ الْجَحِيمَ هِيَ الْمَأْوَىٰ

1. Action: Transgressed boundaries (طَغَىٰ), echoing Pharaoh’s rebellion in 79:17.
2. Priority: Preferred the immediate life of the world (آثَرَ الْحَيَاةَ الدُّنْيَا).
3. Refuge (al-Ma’wa): Hellfire (الْجَحِيمَ) exposed for all who see.

79:40–41 · Path of Reverence Khafa + Naha

وَأَمَّا مَنْ خَافَ مَقَامَ رَبِّهِ وَنَهَى النَّفْسَ عَنِ الْهَوَىٰ ۝ فَإِنَّ الْجَنَّةَ هِيَ الْمَأْوَىٰ

1. Awareness: Feared standing before his Lord (خَافَ مَقَامَ رَبِّهِ), echoing the surah’s thread of khashyah.
2. Self-Restraint: Prevented the soul from unlawful inclination (نَهَى النَّفْسَ عَنِ الْهَوَىٰ).
3. Refuge (al-Ma’wa): Paradise (الْجَنَّةَ).

The Final Hour & Temporal Compression (79:42–46)

When asked “when is its arrival?” (79:42), the Prophet is reminded that finality belongs solely to his Lord (79:44)—and on that Day, an entire lifetime shrinks in perception to either an afternoon or its morning (79:46, echoing duhaha in 79:29).
