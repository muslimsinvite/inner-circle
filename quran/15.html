01 · Structural overview

One unbroken argument across six movements

Surah Al-Hijr opens with the pledge that the Qur'an itself is guarded against alteration (15:9), demonstrates that same precision in the heavens and earth (15:16–25), traces human accountability back to Iblees's refusal (15:26–50), illustrates historical certainty through four communities (15:51–84), and equips the Messenger with spiritual resilience against mockery (15:85–99).

04 · Comparative evidence

False security vs. discerning faith

Chapter 15 contrasts four subjects who rejected messengers or divine command against four models of obedience, mercy, and endurance. Note how both ruined cities of Lot and the Thicket sit on an established, visible road (15:76, 15:79).

Subject & Verses Root Illusion / Sin Warning or Sign Given Decreed Outcome
Iblees
15:28–44 		Arrogance over material origin—refusing to prostrate to a human formed from altered black mud (15:33). Direct divine question and immediate expulsion; reprieved only until the Day of the known time (15:32–38). Cursed until Judgment; has zero authority over Allah's sincere servants (al-mukhlasīn), only over deviators bound for Hell's seven gates (15:40–44).
People of Lot
15:61–77 		Wandering blindly in intoxication (15:72); assaulting Lot's angelic guests and forbidding protection (15:67–70). Lot's repeated ethical appeal ("fear Allah and do not disgrace me," 15:68–71); Lot's family evacuated before dawn without looking back (15:65). Seized by the shriek at sunrise; highest part of the city turned lowest and rained with stones of hard clay—left on an established road for those who discern (15:73–76).
Companions of the Thicket
15:78–79 		Persistent wrongdoing (zālimīn) in defiance of revelation (15:78). Preceding messengers and clear guidance across the shared historical highway (15:10–13, 15:79). Retribution taken from them; both ruined cities stand together on a clear highway (imāmin mubīn, 15:79).
Companions of Al-Hijr (Thamud)
15:80–84 		Denying the messengers, turning away from signs, and carving houses into mountains while feeling untouchably secure (15:80–82). Clear divine signs given directly to them (15:81). The shriek seized them at early morning; none of their wealth or carved fortresses availed them (15:83–84).
15:29–30

The Angels

Prostrated immediately and completely ("all of them entirely") the moment Allah proportioned the human and breathed of His created soul.

15:51–56

Abraham

Greeted with peace despite initial awe; refused despair at old age, affirming that only those astray despair of Allah's mercy (15:56).

15:45–48

The Righteous (Al-Muttaqīn)

Placed in gardens and springs; resentment removed from their chests so they sit as brothers facing one another, free from fatigue forever.

15:85–99

The Messenger

Commanded to forgive graciously, not envy worldly enjoyment, lower his wing to believers, declare openly, and soothe a constricted chest through praise and prostration.
