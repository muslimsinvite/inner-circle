Surah At-Takathur: From Rivalry to Certainty

Across eight concise, percussive verses, Surah At-Takathur traces a complete spiritual arc: how the relentless competition for worldly increase consumes human attention until death, how three escalating warnings shatter complacency, and how intellectual knowledge must ripen into eyewitness certainty before the final questioning over every blessing.
