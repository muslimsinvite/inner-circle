Thesis & structure

Revelation is sent to lift distress, not cause it

Surah Ta-Ha opens by comforting the Prophet Muhammad after intense opposition (20:2) and closes by anchoring the heart in patience and daily praise (20:130). Across its 135 verses, every story tests one core question: will a human being remember Allah's guidance and find peace, or forget and fall into distress?
