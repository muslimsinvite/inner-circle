Surah Maryam: Mercy across generations

Chapter 19 · Verses 1–98 · From private supplication in old age to the cosmic defense of divine unity

Surah Maryam opens with five solitary Arabic letters (Kaf, Ha, Ya, 'Ayn, Sad) and immediately introduces its governing theme: the tender mercy (raḥmah) of God toward His servants. Across 98 verses, the chapter moves through the intimate prayers of Zechariah, the miraculous birth and cradle speech of Jesus, Abraham's gentle yet resolute break from idolatry, and a lineage of prophets—before pivoting to human accountability, the certainty of resurrection, and the promise that the Most Merciful appoints enduring affection (wuddā) for those who believe and do good.
