Thematic Architecture

Six Movements Trace an Arc From Inner Sincerity to Cosmic Judgment

The chapter progresses systematically: establishing exclusive devotion through cosmic signs (1–10), distinguishing the light-filled heart from the hardened heart (11–26), illustrating loyalty through concrete parables (27–41), exposing fair-weather gratitude and false intercession (42–52), calling sinners back from despair (53–67), and unveiling the two processions on the Day of Resurrection (68–75).
