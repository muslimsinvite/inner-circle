01 · Chapter architecture

Three movements shift from praise to supplication

Al-Fatihah unfolds as a deliberate conversation. The first four verses describe Allah in the third person — grounding His name, universal Lordship, dual mercy, and absolute sovereignty. At verse 1:5, the grammar turns directly to the second person (“It is You…”), bridging adoration and need before closing with a shared plural prayer for guidance.
