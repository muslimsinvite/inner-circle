01 · Structural Synthesis

Faith and Everyday Assistance Form a Single Moral Thread

Surah Al-Ma'un unfolds in two mirror movements. Verses 1–3 diagnose the singular denier of the Recompense through two social failures toward the vulnerable. Verses 4–7 shift to plural worshippers whose prayer is undermined by heedlessness, outward show, and refusal of small help.

107:1 · Opening Question Denying the Recompense (يُكَذِّبُ بِالدِّينِ)

The chapter opens by asking the listener to look directly at the moral evidence: “Have you seen the one who denies the Recompense?” Instead of an abstract debate, the next two verses point to visible conduct toward the orphan and the poor.

Dimension Movement I (107:1–3) Movement II (107:4–7)
Opening Cue Rhetorical question: “Have you seen…” Stern warning: “So woe to those who pray”
Grammatical Shift Singular الَّذِي (“the one who”) Plural الَّذِينَ (“those who”)
Inner Defect Denies the Recompense (بِالدِّينِ) Heedless of their prayer (سَاهُونَ) & making show (يُرَاءُونَ)
Social Consequence Drives away the orphan; does not encourage feeding the poor Withholds simple assistance (الْمَاعُونَ)
