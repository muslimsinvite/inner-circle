01 Strategic analysis

Why the “600 years later” claim works only if you accept a secular premise

Understanding the Chronological Snobbery Fallacy before responding.

The common objection

Critics often argue that because Paul wrote his letters in the 1st century—closer in time to Jesus (Isa, peace be upon him)—his accounts must be more accurate than the Quran, which was revealed roughly 600 years later in the 7th century.

The core lie

This argument frames Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) as merely a 7th-century historian trying to piece together 1st-century events from afar, making his account inherently less reliable than someone closer to the era.

The hidden trap: Horizontal Timeline Fallacy

The attacker forces you to play by purely secular historical rules, silently assuming that both the Bible and the Quran are human documents passed down through a centuries-long game of “telephone.” Once you accept that premise, a 600-year gap becomes impossible to defend.

Why people freeze

On the surface, the objection sounds completely logical: in human affairs, an earlier eyewitness outweighs someone born six centuries later. Muslims get caught off guard when they try to defend the historical transmission of 1st-century events instead of anchoring in the core premise of Islam: Divine Revelation (Wahy).

03 Conversational playbook

Four tactical steps for real-life conversations

How to guide a coworker, classmate, or street debater out of the horizontal trap.

Step 01

The Disarming Pivot

Agree to break the premise

Do not get defensive about the 600-year gap. Concede their logic within a purely human framework, then immediately clarify what Islam actually claims.

“You know, if Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) was just a historian relying on human hearsay, you would be 100% right. A 600-year gap would make his account unreliable. But that isn’t what Muslims believe happened.”
Step 02

The Socratic Question

Shift focus to God’s nature

Instead of lecturing, ask a simple question that forces them to test their own theology about whether the Creator is limited by human time.

“Do you believe God is bound by time? If the Creator witnessed the life of Jesus directly, does a 600-year gap affect God’s memory?”
Step 03

The Divine Proofs

Present the vertical vs. horizontal contrast

Now that the premise is exposed, lay out the three pillars: the fractured human chain behind Paul’s letters, the unbroken vertical line of Wahy, and the explicit Quranic declaration in Surah Ali ‘Imran 3:44 (explored in detail below).

“Paul never met Jesus in person, disagreed with direct disciples like Peter and James, and wrote in Greek while Jesus spoke Aramaic. Islam doesn’t rely on a fragile horizontal chain—it relies on an unbroken vertical revelation from the exact same God who witnessed Jesus directly.”
Step 04

The Checkmate

Frame the two real choices

Close the conversation by calmly contrasting the two epistemic options, leaving your listener with a profound theological reality to reflect on.

“So, this isn’t a debate about who was a better historian. It’s a choice between relying on a fragile chain of human letters written by a man who never met Jesus, versus relying on the direct, timeless word of the Creator who created him.”
04 Evidentiary arsenal

Key refutations and the Quranic answer

The historical fragility of the horizontal chain and the direct testimony of Surah Ali ‘Imran.

Proof 01 · The human chain

Three fractures in Paul’s timeline

Even on horizontal historical grounds, the claim of an unproblematic 1st-century transmission breaks down at three points:

  • No eyewitness encounter: Paul never met Jesus (peace be upon him) in person during his earthly ministry.
  • Apostolic disputes: Paul had major theological disputes with the actual disciples who did walk with Jesus—such as Peter (Cephas) and James.
  • Language shift: Jesus spoke Aramaic, whereas Paul wrote his letters in Greek.
Proof 02 · Vertical revelation

Unbroken direct communication

Islamic epistemology does not depend on a chain of 1st-to-7th century human rumor. Instead, it rests on a direct vertical line of communication (Wahy) from the Creator.

The exact same Omniscient God who observed the birth, ministry, and life of Jesus (Isa, AS) is the One who revealed the truth directly to Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ).

Proof 03 · Direct Quranic refutation · Surah Ali ‘Imran (3:44)
“That is from the news of the unseen which We reveal to you, [O Muhammad]. And you were not with them when they cast their pens as to which of them should be responsible for Mary. Nor were you with them when they disputed.”

Why this matters: The Quran itself explicitly anticipates and answers the “you weren’t there” objection regarding the stories of Mary and Jesus. Allah affirms that the Prophet was not physically present—meaning he did not read it in a history book or gather human rumors; Allah is telling him directly from the unseen.
