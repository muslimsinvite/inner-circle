About & Scholarly Sources

Muslims Invite is an interactive Islamic education and Dawah training studio built to help believers and curious seekers study the Quran and respond to modern misconceptions with clarity, wisdom, and primary evidence.

Quranic & Classical Sources

The 114 free Quran study companions pair Arabic script with the Sahih International translation, contextualizing themes through classical Sunni tafsir (Ibn Kathir, al-Tabari, al-Qurtubi) and standard Quranic Arabic morphology.

Upcoming Study Companions

Upcoming interactive companions include original study guides for classical and modern works such as Riyad as-Salihin by Imam Yahya ibn Sharaf an-Nawawi, The Sealed Nectar (Ar-Raheeq Al-Makhtum) by Safi-ur-Rahman al-Mubarakpuri, and The Divine Reality by Hamza Andreas Tzortzis.
